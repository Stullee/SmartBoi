# Full codebase audit — 2026-07-28

Scope: every module in `src/smartboi/`, the analysis scripts, tests, and the
deployment packaging, reviewed for correctness bugs, quant/statistical flaws,
LLM-usage problems, and — the driving question — **what is holding this system
back from producing usable, monetizable results**. Findings were adversarially
verified against the code before being acted on. Everything in "Fixed" below
shipped in the same branch as this document, with regression tests
(251 → 283 tests, all green).

---

## 1. Fixed in this branch

### Critical — wrong results or a dead subsystem

- **EDGAR ingestion crashed on every Finnhub-less deployment**
  (`engine.py` `_extract_relationships`). The Finnhub ticker-search fallback
  was called without a None-guard; any 10-K naming a counterparty EDGAR's own
  lookup couldn't resolve (private companies, brand names — routine) raised
  `AttributeError`, aborted the *entire* hourly EDGAR poll, and — because the
  filing was never dedup-registered — re-billed the paid ~40k-token
  extraction call every cycle. The backfill path looped the same way every
  30 s. Now guarded (matching the already-guarded twin in the candidate
  recheck), per-filing/per-article processing is exception-isolated, and
  backfill retries back off.

- **Relationship backfill marked symbols done when extraction never ran**
  (`engine.py` `_run_relationship_backfill`). A budget-deferred or
  API-failed extraction still wrote `backfill_state`, permanently skipping
  that symbol's 10-K — the propagation graph (the strategy's core mechanism)
  silently stayed empty for it until next year's annual filing. Backfill now
  only completes on a real extraction.

- **A stale SIGNALED status could open a paper trade on a dead thesis**
  (`engine.py`). Evidence merged between signal and entry that dropped the
  dossier below threshold (or flipped its direction) left the status
  SIGNALED, and `_try_open_from_signal` never re-checked — so the 6-hour
  price poll could open a trade far below threshold, possibly in the
  *opposite* direction from the thesis that signaled, against a drift
  baseline snapped for the old direction. Merges that drop below threshold
  now expire the signal, and entry re-validates the signal bar + direction.

- **Supporting evidence LOWERED confidence** (`dossier.py` `_aggregate`).
  Confidence was the *mean* of agreeing items, so a dossier at 0.9 that
  gained a weak 0.2-confidence corroborating item fell to ~0.65 — accumulated
  corroboration moved dossiers *away* from the signal threshold, inverting
  the system's core premise. Now max-plus-corroboration-bonus: agreeing
  evidence can corroborate or be neutral, never penalize; decay still fades a
  lone aging item. **Note: dossier confidences will generally rise on the
  next recompute — the 0.65 threshold was calibrated under the old math, so
  watch the first weeks of signals with that in mind.**

- **The forward-validation dataset silently depended on IB being up**
  (`engine.py`). Daily price marks only ran inside a connected-IB branch and
  the pass was marked done *before* it ran — a Gateway-down day (or an
  exception mid-pass) was a permanently unbackfillable hole in the exact
  dataset that decides whether this strategy works. Price marks now run
  IB-first with Finnhub `/quote` fallback, include **anchors** (widening
  ecosystem benchmarks), and both daily passes mark done only after success.
  Signal-time drift baselines also fall back to Finnhub.

### High — money-relevant measurement bias

- **Ecosystem benchmark included the stock being measured**
  (`forward_returns.py`). Self-inclusion shrank every measured alpha toward
  zero by ~1/N — and to *exactly* zero for one-symbol ecosystems. The
  benchmark is now "the rest of the ecosystem"; unbenchmarkable rows are
  reported, not given a fabricated 0.00. **Alpha numbers will shift; the new
  ones are the real ones.**

- **Close-only stop evaluation flattered the paper record**
  (`paper_journal.py`, `prices.py`). A stock that breached the 8% stop
  intraday and recovered by the close recorded no loss — a real resting stop
  is out. Stops/targets now evaluate against the day's high/low
  (conservative fills: stop at stop-or-worse, target at its level, stop wins
  an ambiguous bar). **Any previously captured win-rate history was computed
  under the old optimistic rule — treat it as upper-bound.**

- **Retries double-paid the LLM and could flip verdicts** (`engine.py`).
  Refuted/not-new outcomes left no marker, so when a *sibling* target of the
  same evidence item was budget-deferred, every retry re-paid
  propose_update + skeptic for targets already done — and a second
  (nondeterministic) skeptic run could accept what the first refuted.
  Retries also re-recorded propagation-cooldown slots for already-merged
  targets, throttling genuinely new propagation with phantom events. Both
  fixed with a per-(target, evidence) handled-outcome cache.

- **Score buckets straddled the trading threshold** (`forward_returns.py`).
  The top bucket was 0.5–1.01 while trades fire at 0.65, so the report
  structurally could not answer "does the region that actually trades beat
  the region just below it?" Buckets now split at 0.65.

- **Statistical overconfidence by construction** (`forward_returns.py`).
  Every daily snapshot counted as an independent observation, but 30
  consecutive rows of one persistent thesis share ~95% of their forward
  window — "hit rate 58% (1,300 theses)" could rest on a few dozen genuinely
  independent windows. The report now prints distinct-symbol counts,
  non-overlapping effective sample sizes (N_eff), symbol-clustered bootstrap
  90% CIs per bucket, and joined-vs-attempted row accounting (so censored
  rows — delistings mid-horizon are disproportionately extreme outcomes —
  are visible instead of silently dropped).

- **LLM scores were single high-temperature samples** (`dossier.py`,
  `skeptic.py`, `graph.py`). Temperature was unset (API default 1.0) on the
  calls whose product gates trades at a hard threshold — some signal
  crossings were sampling noise. All three call sites now run at
  temperature 0, and each evidence record stores the pre-skeptic
  proposal numbers so the skeptic pass's effect is finally measurable.

### Medium — correctness and hygiene

- Undated news articles no longer get a date that slides daily (same story
  was re-billed and re-merged as fresh evidence every day it stayed in
  Finnhub's window); evidence with unparseable dates now decays from merge
  time instead of living at full weight forever.
- Malformed LLM tool output (missing/null fields — the schema is not
  hard-enforced server-side) is validated/clamped instead of crashing the
  poll after the call was paid.
- `signals.jsonl` rows carry an episode key (`signaled_at`) so event-level
  analysis can collapse re-logs of one signal episode.
- Paper journal self-heals the close-crash window that could double-count a
  closed trade after a restart.
- Dashboard "Reset added symbols" ran on a worker thread while the engine
  mutated the same dicts on the event loop (observed-class race:
  `dictionary changed size during iteration`, spec swaps mid-evidence-pass).
  Now runs on the loop.
- Accepted symbols get recommendation refreshes again so the reconcile pass
  can demote a graduated trade target (demote-only — auto-promotion would
  bypass the name-match/seen-count guards).
- 8-K evidence text now uses head+tail truncation instead of `text[:4000]`
  (the first 4k chars of a filing are mostly SEC cover-page boilerplate).
- The "deferred evidence is never lost" claim was false beyond the 3-day
  news / 14-day EDGAR lookback windows — docstring and startup warning now
  say so honestly.

## 2. What still holds back "usable results" (not code bugs — do these next)

Ranked by impact. The unifying insight from the strategy review: **the
trade-level track record can never reach statistical significance at this
signal rate** (at ~3 trades/month, 6 months gives n≈18 → a ±23 pp CI on win
rate; distinguishing 55% from 50% needs ~600 trades). The **daily
dossier-snapshot × price-mark cross-section is the primary evidence engine**
— it scores every dossier every day regardless of signaling, so it doubles as
a threshold counterfactual. Protect it and analyze it first.

1. **Run the capture, uninterrupted, starting now.** Every calendar day
   before price marks accrue is a permanently lost sample. With this branch,
   marks no longer need IB — but the process has to be running. Nothing else
   on this list matters until the clock is running.

2. **Freeze the tradeable universe for a clean 90-day measurement window.**
   Set `AUTO_ACCEPT_TRADEABLES=false` (and consider `AUTO_ACCEPT_MAX_PER_DAY=2`)
   for a quarter. Universe changes mid-sample confound the forward-return
   series, and at up to 5 auto-accepts/day the book can drift ~150
   symbols/month while adding nothing to the dataset you need.

3. **Transaction-cost / liquidity / borrow realism.** Paper fills are
   frictionless daily closes on names down to ~$75M market cap, where a
   realistic round trip costs 1–4% — i.e. 25–50% of the 16% target / 8% stop
   distances. Shorts are simulated symmetrically with no borrow check on
   names that are often hard-to-borrow. Cheapest fix is at analysis time:
   per-side haircuts by market-cap bucket (e.g. 50 bp >$1B, 150 bp
   $300M–1B, 300 bp below) in the forward-return report and trade stats,
   plus a separate "assumes borrow" bucket for sub-$500M shorts.

4. **Signal-event study + decisions ledger.** `signals.jsonl` now has episode
   keys, but nothing analyzes it, and drift-skips/expiries leave only log
   lines. Add a small event study (episode date → forward return from price
   marks) and append drift-skips/expiries to a `decisions.jsonl` with the
   price at decision time — that is the only way to learn whether the
   entry-timing guards help or hurt.

5. **Corroboration is softer than it looks.** Two publishers running the same
   wire story with *reworded* headlines produce two fingerprints and two
   "independent" sources — enough to satisfy `MIN_INDEPENDENT_SOURCES=2`, the
   signal gate. Consider a summary-similarity secondary fingerprint, or
   requiring 3 sources for news-only dossiers.

6. **Universe screen is still flag-only for curated symbols.** The monthly
   screen logs "no longer fits" but nothing demotes a curated-universe
   symbol; a delisted/acquired name keeps accruing dossiers and LLM spend.
   (Runtime-accepted symbols are now reconciled; curated ones are your call —
   surface it on the dashboard at minimum.)

7. **Decision gate at day 90.** If the ≥0.65 bucket shows positive
   benchmark-relative mean at the 20-day horizon with a bootstrap CI
   excluding zero *and* survives the friction haircut → invest in position
   sizing / portfolio construction (correctly absent today — sizing an
   unproven edge is premature). If not, the honest answer is "no demonstrated
   edge yet," and the next lever is **evidence quality** (stated-percentage
   customer-concentration disclosures are the strongest structural bet), not
   threshold tuning.

8. **Smaller items.** An aggregate IWM/sector-ETF benchmark for the whole
   book; fetching 8-K press-release exhibits (Exhibit 99.1) rather than only
   the primary document; a persistent unscored-evidence queue if you ever
   want true zero-loss deferral; the dashboard binds 0.0.0.0 with no auth —
   fine behind HA Ingress, do not port-forward it.

### Timeline expectations (so nobody trades a mirage)

With ~20 directional dossiers snapshotted daily, the cross-section accrues
roughly 40–60 *effective* observations per quarter after overlap and
correlation discounting. A large edge (~3% benchmark-relative per 20 trading
days) becomes detectable in 1–2 quarters; a modest 1% edge needs ~3 years.
The report's N_eff and CI columns are there to keep this honest.

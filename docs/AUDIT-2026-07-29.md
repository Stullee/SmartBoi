## 1. Fixed in this branch

### Paper-record integrity (the reason this system exists)

- **Entries could fill at a stale close from BEFORE the news** (`engine.py`,
  `prices.py`). `_try_open_from_signal` filled at the last daily bar's close.
  When the market is closed — and 8-Ks land predominantly after the bell,
  news polls run around the clock — that close predates the very evidence
  that fired the signal, so the next session's reaction gap was booked as
  P&L no real order could have captured. The drift guard couldn't catch it:
  the baseline was snapshotted from the same stale close. This inflated the
  record precisely when the thesis was right. Now: `PriceBar` carries its
  session date (+ open), and no fill is taken from a bar whose session ended
  before the signal fired. An after-hours/weekend signal defers and fills at
  the **next session's open** (standard next-open convention, recorded as
  `entry_fill="open"`); an intra-session signal still fills at the current
  close (`entry_fill="close"`). Deferral respects the existing entry
  deadline, so a signal can't wait forever.

- **Just-opened trades were stop-evaluated against pre-entry price action**
  (`engine.py` `_mark_and_execute`). A trade opened at the close was
  immediately marked against that same day's full high/low — extremes from
  hours before the position existed. A volatile news day (exactly when
  signals fire) could fabricate a same-tick −1R stop-out no real position
  opened at that price could have experienced. Now: on the entry session, a
  close-filled trade marks against the close only; an open-filled trade owns
  its whole session (entered at the open); from the next session on, the
  full bar applies as before.

- **The ecosystem benchmark mixed ~97 mega-cap anchors into a ~46-name
  small-cap alpha baseline** (`tools.py`, `scripts/analyze_forward_returns.py`).
  Price-marking anchors was a sensible prior-audit fix, but after the anchor
  expansion the equal-weight "ecosystem mean" became dominated by MSFT/GOOGL/
  TSLA-class names — so "benchmark-relative alpha", the day-90 decision
  metric, largely measured the small-cap-vs-mega-cap factor spread in
  whichever direction the regime ran. Benchmarks are now built from
  **tradeable peers only** (anchors stay price-marked — cheap, useful data).

- **Runtime-accepted symbols corrupted ecosystem grouping** (`engine.py`).
  Every accepted candidate got the literal ecosystem `"accepted"`, forming
  one growing cross-sector pseudo-ecosystem whose members were benchmarked
  against each other (a defense supplier against semis and medtech). Accepted
  symbols now inherit the ecosystem of the company whose filing disclosed
  them (`guess_ecosystem`), falling back to `"accepted"` only when no
  discloser is classified.

### Dead subsystems / lost data

- **A transient EDGAR failure permanently cancelled a symbol's backfill**
  (`engine.py`, `edgar.py`). `latest_filing` returned `None` both for "no
  10-K exists" and for a plain HTTP failure; the backfill recorded the
  latter as the former — one SEC 503 at the wrong moment and that symbol's
  10-K was never extracted, leaving it permanently without graph edges (the
  propagation mechanism the whole strategy runs on). `latest_filing` now
  raises on transient failures (symbol stays pending); `None` again means
  "the submissions list was actually inspected and had none". A persistently
  404ing document also now backs off instead of re-fetching every 30 s tick.

- **Extraction and dossier-scoring outcomes were decoupled per filing**
  (`engine.py` `_process_filing`), in both harmful directions: a failed
  extraction alongside successful scoring registered the filing forever
  (its customer/supplier disclosures silently lost until the next annual
  filing); a successful extraction alongside deferred scoring re-paid the
  ~40k-token extraction call — the largest call in the system — on every
  retry. A per-accession `extracted:` marker in the dedup index now tracks
  extraction independently; each accession is extracted exactly once, and
  a registered-but-unextracted filing keeps retrying extraction only.

- **The same filing could double-count a candidate's `seen_count`** (same
  marker fixes it). The backfill and the regular poll each extracted a young
  10-K once apiece, incrementing every named counterparty's `seen_count`
  twice for one physical disclosure — quietly halving the
  `AUTO_ACCEPT_MIN_SEEN_COUNT=2` "repeat disclosure across filings" bar that
  guards auto-accepting TRADE TARGETS. A single mention could auto-accept.

- **Archived dossier history could be clobbered with an empty shell**
  (`engine.py`). Closing a paper trade on a symbol that had been demoted
  (and its dossier archived) recreated an empty live dossier file, which the
  next archive pass moved over the real archived history — permanently
  destroying it. The close path no longer resurrects a dossier whose live
  file is gone, and the archive pass never overwrites an existing archive
  (timestamped sibling instead).

### Correctness / hygiene

- **Ingestion date windows used host-local time** (`engine.py` `_poll_edgar`,
  `_poll_news`) while everything else is UTC. On a US-timezone host (the HA
  deployment target), overnight articles/filings stamped with the new UTC
  date were invisible to every poll for the first 4–8 hours of each UTC day —
  exactly the pre-open window where 8-K-adjacent news matters. Both now UTC.

- **`horizon_days` had no upper clamp** (`engine.py` `_validated_proposal`).
  The tool schema declares max 60, but schemas aren't hard-enforced and this
  codebase has seen live violations. One accepted record with
  `horizon_days=3650` would hold full decay weight for a decade — the daily
  decay pass could never fade that dossier, keeping a stale thesis at the
  signal bar indefinitely. Clamped to `MAX_HORIZON_DAYS`.

- `tzdata` added to dependencies (bar-session honesty needs a correct
  `America/New_York` conversion on slim Docker images).

## 2. Confirmed but NOT fixed in this branch (do these next)

Verified real by the sweep; deferred for budget, in priority order:

1. **Recommendation refresh is inert under stable config** (`engine.py`
   `_run_candidate_ticker_recheck`): market cap/analyst count are only
   re-fetched when the screening *bounds change*, so the reconcile pass can
   never observe post-acceptance drift (a tradeable running to $12B stays
   "tradeable" forever). The comment claims otherwise; a test pins the
   starved behavior. Fix: refresh on age (e.g. >30 days), not only on bounds
   change.
2. **Accepting a candidate never materializes its graph edge**: the filing
   that disclosed the relationship is already dedup-registered, so an
   auto-accepted ANCHOR joins with zero edges and its news propagates to no
   one — the stated purpose of the liberal anchor bar. Fix: on accept, write
   the stored relationship (`related_to`/`rel_types`/`description`) into the
   graph; `graph.add` already dedupes.
3. **Dashboard exposure is worse than documented**: the add-on sets
   `host_network: true`, so the no-auth dashboard — including the mutating
   accept-candidate endpoint and the Finnhub-burning tools endpoints — is
   reachable on the LAN at `:8100` directly, not only via HA Ingress.
   Fine on a trusted home LAN; add a bearer-token option (or gate the POST
   endpoints) before anything else.
4. **Two signal-episode terminations write no `decisions.jsonl` row**
   (direction flip while a trade is open; symbol archived while SIGNALED) —
   the event study mislabels these "untracked". Low impact, cheap fix.
5. **Config drift between deployment paths**: `.env.example` says
   `MAX_HORIZON_DAYS=56` / screen bounds 100–5000/6; the add-on's
   `config.yaml` ships 21 and 75–5000/10. Whichever is intended, they should
   agree — the two deployment paths currently run different strategies.

## 3. What still blocks money (strategy review)

The critic's ranked list — grounded in this checkout's actual code and
universe (46 tradeables, 29 marked "unverified", 97 anchors, 9 ecosystems):

1. **The propagation graph is structurally idle for most of the universe.**
   Only DCO has live seeded edges; anchor-side extraction yields nothing by
   design (giants don't name their small suppliers), and many concentration
   disclosures are anonymized ("Customer A, 23% of sales") so extraction
   finds no edge. Until edges exist, tradeables can only accumulate rare
   direct news on deliberately thin-coverage names — the most likely day-90
   outcome becomes "not enough data". **Action (a few hours, highest ROI):**
   hand-seed `SEED_RELATIONSHIPS` for the stated-percentage relationships
   already documented in `universe.py` (AXL→GM, STRT→GM/F/STLA,
   SUP→GM/F/Toyota, THRM→OEMs, PUMP→Permian operators, CDRE/VVX/RDW→defense
   primes); reading the 10-K concentration table yourself is the same
   information the extractor would read — guaranteed coverage at window
   start, no look-ahead. Then track **edges-per-tradeable** as a top health
   metric, and swap out any tradeable whose 10-K names no concentrated
   customer (it can never receive propagated evidence).

2. **Decide the scoring model NOW and freeze one release.** All three LLM
   roles run claude-haiku-4-5 (cheapest tier) on the calls whose numbers
   gate trades, while the cost math says Sonnet-tier scoring adds only
   ~$70–150/month. Model provenance correctly treats any swap as a
   track-record reset, and Anthropic snapshot lifetimes (~16–22 months
   historically; Haiku 4.5 is ~10 months old) mean **this apparatus can only
   ever validate a LARGE edge (~3%/20d, detectable in 1–2 quarters) within
   one model's lifetime** — the "run 3 years to find a 1% edge" path does
   not exist. Run ~20 recent evidence items through Haiku vs Sonnet offline,
   pick, then ship ONE final release bundling: model choice, seeded edges,
   this branch's fixes, `AUTO_ACCEPT_TRADEABLES=false`, and a frozen anchor
   set (anchor churn moves both the benchmark and the propagation-source set
   mid-sample). Then hands off for 90 days.

3. **The day-90 gate as previously written is vacuous.** At ~3 signals/
   month, the ≥0.65 bucket cannot produce a usable bootstrap CI in 90 days
   (the code returns None under 2 symbols; it's statistical fiction under
   ~8). Pre-register — today, in a dated doc, before anyone knows the
   answer — a gate that CAN fire at realistic n: (i) positive score→20d
   benchmark-relative rank correlation with symbol-clustered CI excluding 0,
   (ii) top two buckets jointly beat the bottom two by >2× bucketed
   round-trip friction, (iii) sign consistent in ≥6 of 9 ecosystems. PROCEED
   to sizing only if all three; FOLD/PIVOT to evidence quality if
   correlation ≤0 or friction isn't cleared. Add the three lines to
   `analyze_forward_returns` output so the read is mechanical.

4. **The skeptic rules on "already priced in" without ever seeing a price.**
   The one factual input that decides most propagated evidence — has the
   target already moved since the origin event — sits in `price_marks.jsonl`
   and never enters the prompt. This is the false-positive channel that
   survives every existing defense (real fact, real relationship, target
   already repriced). Inject two computed lines into the skeptic/updater
   prompts (target return since evidence date; ecosystem mean over the same
   window) — zero extra API calls, ~60 tokens. Also store stated revenue-%
   figures as numeric edge fields, and surface skeptic accept/adjust/refute
   rates in diagnostics (if accept-rate is ~100%, the adversarial layer is
   decorative).

5. **LLM spend is a non-blocker; the budget's failure mode is the real
   risk.** Realistic all-in cost at Haiku: ~$30–60/month (hard cap ~$300–450;
   Sonnet-tier scoring ~$90–180 realistic). Against any account >$25k this
   is noise. The REAL risk is inverted: on a news-heavy day the 3000-call
   cap defers evidence, deferred items age past the 3-day news lookback,
   and they become permanently unscored — an invisible hole in the dataset,
   with no alert. Add a loud budget-exhausted alert + a deferred-then-
   expired counter.

6. **Price marks ARE backfillable — and nothing alarms on silence.** Daily
   closes are public, reproducible data; a week of downtime currently
   invalidates ~5 weeks of joinable cross-section for no reason. Build
   `scripts/backfill_price_marks.py` (rows flagged `"backfilled": true`),
   and a dead-man's switch: POST the existing ~10-min heartbeat to the
   webhook and alert after ~3h of silence via HA automation. (Dossier
   snapshots remain genuinely unbackfillable — uptime still matters.)

7. **Finnhub free tier is NOT binding** (verified: 143-symbol hourly pass ≈
   2.6 min of the rate budget; 15-min delay irrelevant at a weeks-long
   horizon). Don't spend on data until an edge shows.

8. **Honest expectations, written down.** The underlying anomaly
   (Cohen-Frazzini) decayed post-publication and is systematically harvested
   in liquid names; the defensible residual is exactly the sub-$500M
   thin-coverage tail — where 150–300bp/side friction and hard borrow
   consume most of gross, and strategy capacity is roughly ~$1M. An honest
   prior that this pipeline has net-positive detectable alpha is maybe
   10–20%. Running cost ~$100–500/quarter buys a clean falsification of
   that hypothesis — a fair trade **only if the fold rule is honored**.
   Commit now: no real capital before the pre-registered gate passes;
   capital sized to capacity if it does; a "cannot tell" at day 90 means
   the evidence-quality levers (items 1 and 4) plus one more pinned-model
   quarter — not silent drift.
